JKLab

Warehouse of Public Key Infrastruct JKLab.

JKLab is a technology development brand dedicated to researching PKI and cryptographic technologies and creating practical software tools. Leveraging expertise in certificates, digital signatures, encryption, and security protocols, we aim to resolve technical challenges faced by developers and provide efficient development environments. We continuously research and develop PKI development tools and cryptography-related solutions, including our flagship project, BerEditor. By bridging specialized technology with practical tools, JKLab supports the development of security software that is both more convenient and efficient.

Features

PKI Technology

PKI (Public Key Infrastructure) is a security framework that utilizes public-key cryptography to verify the identities of users and systems, while ensuring data confidentiality, integrity, authentication, and non-repudiation. It serves as a core foundational technology for internet communication, digital signatures, digital certificate management, and corporate security systems.

BerEditor

BerEditor is an integrated GUI tool designed to support developers working with PKI and cryptographic technologies. It facilitates the development of security protocols and troubleshooting by analyzing and validating ASN.1 encoded data, digital certificates, digital signatures, and various other PKI-related data.

CertMan

CertMan is a certificate management tool that supports the management and verification of digital certificates and Public Key Infrastructure (PKI). It enables users to examine certificate issuance details, validity periods, public keys, extension fields, and certificate chains, while facilitating the efficient execution of technical tasks related to certificate management.

CryptokiMan

CryptokiMan is a tool designed to support the management and development of cryptographic devices and tokens based on the PKCS#11 standard. It interfaces with Hardware Security Modules (HSMs), smart cards, and PKCS#11-compliant cryptographic modules, enabling users to verify and test cryptographic objects, key pairs, cryptographic operations, and digital signature functions.
